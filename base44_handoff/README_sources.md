# Base44 handoff: Jezero Marswalk data

Generated by `routing/export_handoff.py` from the team's processed NASA layers. Nothing in these files was typed by hand. To regenerate:
`python3 -m routing.export_handoff --stops curated/proposed_stops.csv --option A --base-lat 18.452739 --base-lon 77.405768 --out base44_handoff`

## Coordinates (read this first)
- Every point, route and layer uses **`east_m`, `north_m`**: ground metres from the habitat (habitat = 0, 0). Draw with these.
- Each point also includes `lat`/`lon` (planetocentric, east-positive, Mars 2000 sphere R = 3,396,190 m) and `x_m`/`y_m` (DTM equirectangular projection), so a check against another map is possible.
- This is **not** Earth GeoJSON (RFC 7946). Routes use `"type": "LineString"`, but the coordinates are `[east_m, north_m]`.
- PNG layers are north-up and all share the same bounds, listed in `layers_manifest.json` (`bounds.east_m`, `bounds.north_m`). Transparent pixels mean no data.
- `alignment_check.png` was drawn **only** from these files; if your app looks the same, the alignment is correct.

## Files
| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| mission_config.json | habitat, study area, grid, EVA limits (6 h recommended / 8 h max / 2 km), slope policy, CRS |
| waypoints.json | habitat, 6 science stops (with Perseverance photo sol/site_drive), 6 candidate terrain shelters |
| minerals.json | high-confidence CRISM patches (carbonate, olivine, clay) near the habitat: centre, bbox, area, linked stops |
| routes.json | round trips habitat → stop → habitat (fastest / easiest / Pareto), plus a tour of all 6 stops; each has segments of about 50 m |
| emergency.json | 4 scenarios (from the two farthest stops with 1 h and 2 h of suit supply), destinations ranked by oxygen need |
| layers_manifest.json + map_layers/ | hillshade, elevation, slope, roughness, thermal inertia, minerals, sky blocked, comms line of sight |
| route_model.json | equations and sources for time, effort and oxygen |
| conditions.json | radiation summary (Curiosity RAD, last 30 days). Weather is pending (MEDA) |

## Important for the app
1. **There is no hard slope limit.** Steeper terrain costs more, so the planner avoids it. Any route length above 20° is listed under `warnings`. The carbonate routes cross 107–190 m of terrain steeper than 20° (up to 28°), because the carbonates sit on top of an escarpment. Show that warning.
2. **Oxygen is not verified** (`oxygen_verified: false`). `relative_effort` is a model value, "hours of suit supply at 1200 BTU/h". Label it as an estimate.
3. Shelters are **terrain only**: places where hills block the most sky, on walkable ground at 20° or less. They have no oxygen and no comms.
4. Minerals are **probable** orbital detections, not confirmed samples.
5. Radiation was measured in **Gale crater**, not at Jezero. Use it as a reference value only.
6. Masked areas: cells without elevation, thermal inertia below 100 (there are none in this area), and the DTM artefact zone west of lon 77.35 E (an assumption from our own inspection; it is far from this area and does not change any route).
7. Each route's `total_time_min` already includes 30 min of science at the stop.
8. **Two different slopes** (update Nov 14): `max_slope_deg` is the walking grade, in the direction of travel. `max_terrain_slope_deg` is the steepest slope of the ground under the route, which the route may cross sideways. The carbonate routes cross ground of 34–40° diagonally, so the walking grade stays at 28° or less. Show both with different names. Definitions are in `routes.json` → `field_definitions`.
9. **Photos** (update Nov 14): each science stop in `waypoints.json` has `photos`, with 2 raw Perseverance images (Mastcam-Z and Navcam). Each photo has `image_url` (official NASA download link), `credit`, sol, date and the distance from the rover position to the stop. The files with the same names are also in Ronald's Drive. Always show the credit, and note that the camera direction is not confirmed (`photo_note`).
10. **Weather:** only pressure (MEDA, sols 417/420/435/674/708): per-sol median/min/max and hourly medians in `conditions.json`, and `pressure_pa` on each stop. There is no wind data: MEDA has no published wind data after sol 315.

## Sources
- Elevation, slope, roughness: USGS Mars 2020 Terrain Relative Navigation HiRISE DTM (1 m), resampled to 5 m.
- Thermal inertia: THEMIS mosaic of Jezero (Edwards et al.), Zenodo 4931348.
- Minerals: CRISM mineral map of Jezero (Parente, Itoh, Saranathan), Zenodo 5575824.
- Stops and photos: NASA Mars 2020 rover waypoints and raw images (stop selection by Ronald).
- Radiation: MSL RAD Level 2, NASA CDAWeb.
- Route model: Tobler hiking function; Apollo 3.6 km/h walkback speed (Apollo Lunar Surface Journal); Pandolf equation (NASA NTRS 20240001423); 1200 BTU/h EVA planning rate (ICES-2026-127); 20° suit reference (NASA Artemis, Coan 2021, NTRS 20210000827).

11. **New (Nov 14, afternoon):**
    - **Every route segment** now includes `nearest_safe_place_id`, `time_to_nearest_safe_place_min` and `max_time_to_nearest_safe_place_min`: the habitat or the terrain shelter you can reach fastest from that segment. No point on any route is more than 7.5 min from a safe place.
    - **"I have this much time"** mode: `routes.json` → `time_budget_plans` lists, for 1, 1.5, 2, 3, 4, 6 and 8 h, which stops fit (most stops, then least effort) and the `route_id` of the matching `multi_stop` route. With 4 h or more, all 6 stops fit (3 h 57 min).
    - **Shelters as intermediate stops:** for each stop there is a `via_shelter` route (habitat → shelter → stop → habitat) with `extra_time_vs_easiest_min` and `extra_effort_vs_easiest` compared with the direct route.

12. **Perseverance on the map** (new): `rover_positions.json` has the rover's 97 end-of-drive positions inside the area (sols 402–736), each with sol, `site_drive` and coordinates. The 6 positions where the stop photos were taken also carry `photos`. Each photo in `waypoints.json` now has `taken_from` (the point where the rover was standing), so the app can draw a camera marker there and a line to the stop. `rover_traverse.json` (prepared by Ronald from NASA's M20_traverse.json) has the full drive path inside the area: 100 drives (sols 402–753) as LineStrings in east_m/north_m with exact, not grid-snapped, coordinates. All 97 rover positions lie within a few metres of it. For the label of the photo-to-stop line, use `distance_photo_position_to_stop_m` from the package, not the distance between the drawn points (the points are snapped to 5 m cells, so they can differ by a few metres; e.g. olivine_01 270.7 vs 275 m).

13. **Radiation estimate (new):** every route has `radiation_dose_estimate_uGy` (walking time × Curiosity RAD median rate × (1 − sky hidden)); each shelter has `dose_rate_estimate_uGy_per_h` and `dose_saving_vs_open_sky_pct` (18–21%). It is an **estimate** for comparing routes and shelters: the rate is from Gale crater, and terrain shielding is approximated geometrically (`route_model.json` → `radiation`). Time at stops is not included.
